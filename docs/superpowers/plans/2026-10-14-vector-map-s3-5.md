## Goal and stop line

One named fabrication preset, `relief-0.4`, selected explicitly with `--preset relief-0.4` or a recipe
`preset` field. It fills the preset precedence slot that S3.4 left empty: built-in defaults, preset, recipe,
explicit CLI. It carries fabrication values only: silhouette thickness S = 1.2 mm, nominal structure width
0.8 mm, structure height H = 0.6 mm, nominal detail width 0.5 mm, detail height or depth D = 0.2 mm, backing
disabled. Every value stays overridable by recipe and CLI. The resolved values reach the manifest and the
layered replay recipe, and the manifest `relief.preset` names the preset.

Stop at review-ready S3.5. No second preset, no preset-supplied VTracer or Canny values, no change to the
S1.4 fitting defaults, no mesh render or CAD acceptance (S3.6), no print-suitability claim, and no geometry
change in the S3.4 source builder. A run without a preset keeps today's behavior exactly, including
`relief.preset: null`.

## Gates

Mario ruled on all five points in the PR #99 review. Gates 1–4 are approved as proposed. Gate 5 is rewritten
below against the review finding.

1. **A preset requires an export. Approved.** An effective `--preset` or recipe `preset` with no effective
   `export_scad` exits 2, naming the missing export. The preset describes the SCAD source; with no source its
   values are dormant, and the existing dormant-value rule already rejects operator-supplied dormant values.
   A preset run without export would otherwise need an exception that weakens that rule.
   The S3.4 opt-out is preserved and stated here explicitly: an explicit `--no-export-scad` over a recipe
   `export_scad: true` validates every value, preset values included, and writes no SCAD. Both paths are
   covered by tests, the plain missing-export rejection and the opt-out that keeps the validated values.
2. **The preset never decides role selection. Approved.** The preset contributes a role width or a role height
   only when that role is in the selection. `--layers silhouette --preset relief-0.4 --export-scad` is valid:
   it publishes silhouette only, and structure and detail heights stay null. Without this rule a preset would
   trip two existing checks, `HEIGHT_ROLES` in `_relief()` ("structure_height_mm was given, but structure is
   not selected") and the role admission in `_role_requests()` ("structure settings were given, but structure
   is not selected"). The operator did not supply those values, so neither is an operator error.
   The S3.4 rule is unchanged for operator values: an explicit height for a role absent from the selection
   still exits 2, preset or no preset. Tests cover both cases, a preset-only silhouette selection and an
   explicit absent-role override under a preset.
3. **Preset field set. Approved, both nominal widths included.** The preset supplies `silhouette_thickness_mm`,
   `structure_height_mm`, `detail_height_mm`, `backing_mm`, `structure.width_mm`, and `detail.width_mm`. It
   supplies no `export_scad`, `center`, `detail_mode`, `vtracer`, Canny control, or role source. A preset run
   therefore keeps detail mode `raised`, centering off, and the S1.4 fitting defaults. The two nominal band
   widths are raster-preparation settings; their low-width warnings still come from the existing four-pixel
   rule.
4. **CLI override surface. Approved.** `--preset` takes one known name; an unknown name exits 2 and names
   `relief-0.4` as the only preset. `--preset` replaces a recipe `preset`, because the CLI is the last layer.
   No `--no-preset` exists, matching the Sprint 3 decision that a recipe preset cannot be cleared from the
   CLI while `relief-0.4` is the only preset. The name matches exactly, with no case folding or aliases.
5. **Manifest and replay. Revised.** `relief.preset` holds the preset name, or null when none was selected,
   and the seven resolved relief values are recorded in the layered replay recipe. The first draft kept role
   widths out of the replay recipe on the assumption that replay never expands bands. That assumption is
   false once a preset is operative: `_merge_role()` keeps a lower `width_mm` when the replay role switches to
   `source: map`, and `_prepare_role()` then calls `_expand()` on a mask that already carries its width. The
   replay contract below makes the bypass explicit.

## Replay contract

The replay recipe records the run faithfully and prepares nothing a second time.

- The recipe carries the operative `preset` field and the seven resolved relief values: `export_scad`,
  `center`, `detail_mode`, `backing_mm`, `silhouette_thickness_mm`, `structure_height_mm`, and
  `detail_height_mm`. Relief values resolve to the same numbers through the normal chain, because the recipe
  layer sits above the preset layer and records what the preset resolved to.
- Each optional role object records its resolved `width_mm` and gains `prepared: true`. Recording the width
  keeps the prepared value visible; `prepared` is what stops the second expansion.
- `prepared: true` is a role field that states the mask at `path` is already at its prepared width, so
  preparation performs no `_expand()` call and computes no expansion record for that role. It requires
  `source: map`; `prepared` with a Canny source exits 2. It joins `gap_close_mm` as a recipe-only role field
  with no CLI flag, so the operator surface is unchanged. An operator writing a recipe by hand may use it for
  a mask that is already at width.
- A prepared role emits no `requested_width_below_four_pixels` warning, because no expansion runs and the
  warning describes an expansion that did not happen. The role manifest records `width_mm` as before and
  `expansion: null`, which is the observable difference from a widening run. The manifest gains no `prepared`
  field; the null expansion is the signal.
- Rejected alternative: replaying with `width_mm: 0` to mean "no expansion". Zero is not a positive finite
  length today and exits 2, and making zero a meaningful width would change the CLI surface for a replay
  concern.
- Replay evidence is its own test. Replay the debug recipe of a preset run and compare the prepared masks
  byte for byte with the original prepared masks, compare the published layer SVGs with the original bundle's
  layer SVGs, and compare the resolved relief objects. The preset-versus-explicit-flags SCAD equality test
  stays, but it does not exercise replay and is not a substitute.

## Preset contents and precedence

| Field | Value | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| `silhouette_thickness_mm` | 1.2 mm | Relief number, S |
| `structure.width_mm` | 0.8 mm | Nominal structure band width, used in raster preparation |
| `structure_height_mm` | 0.6 mm | Relief number, H |
| `detail.width_mm` | 0.5 mm | Nominal detail band width, used in raster preparation |
| `detail_height_mm` | 0.2 mm | Relief number, D |
| `backing_mm` | 0.0 | Disabled backing |

Resolution rules:

- The preset layer is built only when a preset is selected. Without a selection the layer is `{}`, exactly as
  the entry point passes it today.
- Layers merge in order, so a recipe value replaces a preset value field by field, and an explicit CLI value
  replaces both. `--silhouette-thickness-mm 1.5` with `--preset relief-0.4` yields S = 1.5 mm.
- A preset supplies only the fields above. `--no-export-scad`, `--no-center`, and `--detail-mode raised` behave
  as they do now, since the preset sets none of them.
- Merged values pass through the existing relief checks. The preset values are valid on their own: D < S for
  engraving holds, and backing stays zero or positive.
- The preset carries no VTracer control and no Canny threshold or blur. Fitting defaults come from S1.4 and
  stay unchanged.
- `--preset relief-0.4 --detail-mode engraved` is valid with no special case: 0.2 mm is above zero and below
  1.2 mm.
- Precedence is unchanged for every non-preset field. The preset never sets `input`, `input_kind`,
  `width_mm`, `height_mm`, `layers`, or a role source.

## Admission and interaction rules

- A preset needs `input_kind image` with an explicit selection that includes silhouette. Standalone mask and
  edge modes reject `--preset` with exit 2, on the same rule that already rejects `--export-scad` and relief
  settings there.
- An image run without explicit layers rejects a preset with exit 2, naming the missing selection.
- A preset with effective export requires a positive silhouette thickness after resolution. A selected preset
  always supplies one, and no later layer can clear a supplied value, so the missing-thickness error is
  unreachable on a preset run. The check still fires for a run without a preset, which is the path that keeps
  the no-implicit-preset rule observable.
- Slot assignment is untouched: silhouette records S with mode `base`, structure records H with mode `raised`,
  and detail records D with mode `raised` or `engraved`.
- Warning behavior is untouched, with one addition from the replay contract: multiple-solids, thin-material,
  feature-coverage, and low-width warnings keep their current triggers and text, and a role whose mask is
  already prepared emits no low-width warning because no expansion runs.
- No implicit preset exists. A layered export run without a preset and without heights still exits 2 with
  "SCAD export requires a positive silhouette_thickness_mm", and a run without export still publishes
  `relief.preset: null`.

## Manifest, result and replay recipe

`Settings` gains `preset: str | None = None`, and the resolved relief mapping from `_relief()` in
`vector_map_config.py` carries it. `relief.preset` in the layered manifest reports the name, or null.

The layered replay recipe follows the replay contract above: `preset`, the seven resolved relief fields, and one
role object per selected optional role carrying its resolved `width_mm` and `prepared: true`. A replay of that
recipe resolves to the same values through the normal precedence chain, because the recipe layer sits above the
preset layer and the recorded values equal the resolved ones, and it expands no prepared mask.

Unchanged: schema version 1, the standalone manifest shape, the standalone result shape, `artifacts.scad` and
its hash, the fixed SCAD ownership rules, and the manifest-last publication. The result JSON gains no field.
The role manifest keeps its current fields; a prepared role reports `expansion: null`.

## Documentation

`scripts/USAGE.md` is the only operator surface. It gains `--preset` in the flags table, the preset table, the
explicit-selection requirement, the override order, and the recorded manifest and replay fields. It replaces
the sentence that `preset` stays null until a later release. It states that the preset describes fabrication
settings only, that its values do not guarantee suitability for a given 0.4 mm nozzle, extrusion width,
material, or slicer configuration, and that suitability needs separate evidence. It states that fitting
defaults come from S1.4, not the preset.

Two code comments that name S3.5 as pending are updated: the entry-point note beside the empty preset layer,
and the `_relief()` docstring in `vector_map_artifacts.py`. Merged plan documents stay as written; the S3.4
plan is a historical record and is not edited.
